Why We Built This

Too many teams waste time chasing status updates or taking vague "done" messages on faith. We built Tasko to eliminate accountability friction—turning task assignment, daily routines, and proof verification into one transparent, automated system.

What Tasko Is & Why It's Useful

Tasko is an all-in-one workspace manager powered by Flask, SQLite, JWT security, and Gemini AI. CEOs and managers can assign prioritized tasks with deadlines, run daily checklists that auto-reset every 24 hours, switch between List and Kanban views, manage custom team stances and permissions, chat live, and let Gemini AI automatically audit uploaded proof images with an instant pass/fail verdict and explanation.

Agencies & Companies That Should Use It

  • Creative, Marketing & Client Agencies: Verify campaign deliverables, design handoffs, and daily client operations without constant check-ins.
  • Software & Product Teams: Keep sprint tasks, bug fixes, and daily standup proofs organized in Kanban or list views.
  • Operations, Retail & Field Teams: Enforce opening/closing checklists and mandatory 24-hour routines with visual photo proof.
  • Fast-Growing Startups & SMBs: Onboard staff via Workspace ID, approve new hires, control role permissions, and keep a complete audit trail.
